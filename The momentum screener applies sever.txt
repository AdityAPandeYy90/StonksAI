The momentum screener applies several filters sequentially to filter the universe down to the strongest stocks. The exact filters and their configurations (defined in 
settings.yaml
 and applied in 
filters.py
) are as follows:

1. Price Filter
Requirement: Stock price must be greater than or equal to a minimum threshold.
Filter Rule: Close >= min_price
Default Value: 100.0 INR (keeps stocks priced at or above Rs. 100)
2. Liquidity Filter
Requirement: 50-day average daily dollar volume must meet a minimum liquidity threshold.
Filter Rule: Avg_Dollar_Volume_Cr >= min_dollar_volume_cr
Default Value: 75.0 Crores (average daily trading volume value of at least Rs. 75,000,000)
3. Volatility Filter
Requirement: Average True Range (ATR) as a percentage of the stock price must meet a minimum volatility threshold.
Filter Rule: ATR_pct >= min_atr_percent
Default Value: 2.5% (filters out low-volatility / slow-moving stocks)
4. Trend (Uptrend) Filters
Requirement: The stock's price must be in a strong uptrend, trading at least 1% above its key moving averages.
Filter Rules:
Close > EMA21 * 1.01 (price is > 1% above the 21-day Exponential Moving Average)
Close > SMA50 * 1.01 (price is > 1% above the 50-day Simple Moving Average)
Close > SMA200 * 1.01 (price is > 1% above the 200-day Simple Moving Average)
5. Watchlist Slicing & Selection Filter
After the filtering steps in 
filters.py
, the remaining universe is ranked (using 
rank_universe_momentum
) and sliced into watchlists in 
export.py
:

Top Percentage Filter: Keeps only the top 7% of the remaining stocks (default configured as top_percent: 7).
Slices Generated:
Combined Momentum: Top 7% sorted by Momentum_Score (weighted average of 1M, 3M, and 6M percentile ranks: 25% 1M, 35% 3M, 40% 6M).
1M Momentum: Top 7% sorted by Rank1M (1-month return percentile rank).
3M Momentum: Top 7% sorted by Rank3M (3-month return percentile rank).
6M Momentum: Top 7% sorted by Rank6M (6-month return percentile rank).
Intersection: A highly selective list containing only stocks that appear in the top 7% of all three lists (1M, 3M, and 6M) concurrently.
