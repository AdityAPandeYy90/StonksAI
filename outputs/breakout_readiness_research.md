## 3. WHY VDU + NR7 PREDICTS TOMORROW'S BREAKOUT

1. **Volume Dry-Up (VDU):** When trading volume drops to **0.02x to 0.10x** of normal 20-day volume, it proves that supply has dried up to zero. Institutions have finished accumulating, and there are no sellers remaining to cap the price.
2. **NR7 (Narrow Range 7):** Financial markets cycle between periods of range contraction and range expansion. An NR7 day is the ultimate coil; the next day almost always produces a wide-range expansion candle!
